Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Porta is a non-custodial account-abstraction wallet. Keys stay on your device. This policy explains what Porta and porta.finance receive when you use the product and this site.

Who we are

DracoLabs Ltd operates Porta and porta.finance. Contact: hello@porta.finance.

Local keys

Creating or importing a wallet happens on your device. We do not hold your seed phrase, private keys, or assets. We cannot sign, freeze, or reverse a transaction for you.

What we collect

Support — if you email hello@porta.finance, we receive your address and the message so we can reply. Google sign-in — if you authenticate with Google, we receive the email and basic profile Google shares for that OAuth consent, and we use it to sign you in. We do not get your Google password. Telegram Mini App — if you open Porta in Telegram, Telegram hosts that surface; we do not receive your Telegram password. Hosting and mail — GitHub Pages and our email provider process the technical data they need to serve the site and deliver mail.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. This site does not run an ad or analytics pixel. We will never ask for your seed phrase.

On-chain data

Transfers you make on public production networks are public. PortaWallet supports seven production networks. That record lives on those networks, not in a Porta database of your holdings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date on this page.
